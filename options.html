ThreadLedger

ThreadLedger is ready. Open a Gmail conversation and click Inventory this thread. The button is on the conversation toolbar, in the popup, or at the bottom right if Gmail's toolbar cannot be found.

First line length

Off by default. Quoted history and “On … wrote:” blocks are skipped so the first line is the new text.

The original Gmail tooltip is always kept in its own column. If a tooltip cannot be parsed, the ISO cell is left blank.

One suggestion per line. The table also offers categories you have already typed.

Saved notes

Categories and notes are stored in Chrome on this device, one set per Gmail thread. Clearing them does not change Gmail.

Privacy

ThreadLedger reads the Gmail conversation you already have open: sender, recipients, the date tooltip, subject, attachment names, and the first line of the visible message. It reads that page in your browser. It does not sign in to Google, it does not call the Gmail API, and it does not open a connection to send the mail anywhere.

There is no ThreadLedger account and no tracking. Messages, categories, and notes stay in local storage on this computer. Copy and download stay on your machine. The only outbound link is the optional Drive I/O link, and it opens only when you click it. It does not include email content.
